# The Best Meal You Ever Had

`My grandmother's hand-rolled gnocchi in her tiny Bologna kitchen, steam rising as she pressed each dumpling against the fork, tasting of butter, sage, and Sunday patience.`

This memory matters because it captures a moment of pure attention — my grandmother teaching me patience through food, one dumpling at a time. The concrete sensory details make it real: the steam, the fork tines, the butter and sage. It's not just about eating; it's about who I was with and how I felt.
